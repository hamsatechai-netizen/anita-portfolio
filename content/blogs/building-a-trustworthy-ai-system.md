Trustworthy AI requires more than model accuracy. It depends on understandable reasoning, performance under uncertainty, meaningful control over personal data, fairness, reliability, usefulness, and usability.

This article brings those qualities together through three connected layers:

- **Technical pillars:** explainability, privacy preservation, and security
- **Ethical pillars:** accountability, fairness, compliance, and sustainability
- **Responsible governance:** continuous oversight and adaptation across the AI lifecycle

The central argument is that governance is not a one-time approval. Trust must be maintained as systems, data, risks, and social expectations change.
