As AI agents gain the ability to decide, act, and learn, human oversight cannot be treated as an optional interface feature.

This article uses a simple analogy - trusting a driver with someone you care about - to ask what people need before they can trust an autonomous system. The answer includes a visible route, timely status updates, a way to pause or redirect the system, and clear boundaries around decisions that should remain human.

The central argument is that autonomy should support human judgment, not make it invisible. Responsible agentic systems need:

- Visibility into what the system is doing
- Meaningful updates at decision points
- Human override and escalation paths
- Emotional and ethical boundaries
